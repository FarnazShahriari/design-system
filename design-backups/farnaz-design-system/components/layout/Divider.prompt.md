A block of nothing at section rhythm, between two same-coloured sections.

```jsx
<Section theme="muted">…</Section>
<Divider theme="muted" />
<Section theme="muted">…</Section>
```

Props: `theme?: "light" | "muted" | "dark" | "accent" | "inherit"; size?: "sm" | "default" | "lg"; className?: string;`
